IMAGES
======

Put your images here. The app references these exact filenames:

1. logo.png            → the "BSIT 1E DEAL OR NO DEAL" watermark shown at the
                        bottom-left of the TV homepage. Recommended ~400px wide
                        (transparent background works best).

2. ginoong-ricky.png   → the teacher's face inside the dino game circle (square
                        image works best; it is cropped to a circle).

If a file is missing, the app gracefully hides it (logo) or shows a placeholder
emoji (dino face) instead.
